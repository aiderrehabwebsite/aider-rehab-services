About AIDER

Support that understands every child.

AIDER Rehab Services is a child-focused psychological and developmental support centre founded by Greeshma Saravanan, Founder & Lead Psychologist | Play Therapist.

Established in October 2021, AIDER works with children, adolescents, parents and families in a warm, supportive and child-friendly environment.

With centres at Vadanappalli and Kara (Kodungallur), AIDER provides both offline and online services, making professional support more accessible to families.

Our approach is centred on understanding each child as an individual and developing support based on their unique needs, strengths and goals.

Our Programs

Structured programs for children and families.

AIDER offers structured programs designed around the developmental and educational needs of children and families.

PROGRAM 01

Early Developmental Program

For young children who need support with developmental skills, communication, behaviour, sensory needs, self-help skills and school readiness.

PROGRAM 02

Educational Development Program

Focused on children experiencing difficulties with reading, writing, basic academic skills, attention, memory and learning habits.

PROGRAM 03

STUDYHUB

Homework Support & Exam Preparation.

  • Independent homework habits
  • Study skills
  • Attention and focus
  • Language and mathematics skills
  • Logic and memory
  • Better learning routines
  • Healthy screen and mobile-use habits
PROGRAM 04

Sensory Skill Training

Activities designed to support sensory processing, attention, body awareness and participation in everyday activities.

PROGRAM 05

Vacation Programs

Structured and engaging activities during school vacations, combining learning, developmental activities, social interaction and therapeutic support.

PROGRAM 06

Group Therapy Programs

Small-group activities that encourage communication, social interaction, cooperation, emotional development and functional skills.

PROGRAM 07

Parent Workshops

Practical sessions helping parents understand child development, behaviour, learning and effective ways of supporting their children at home.

PROGRAM 08

Teenage Workshops

Age-appropriate programs addressing emotional development, social skills, study habits, self-awareness, communication and other challenges faced during adolescence.

Greeshma Saravanan is a Consultant Psychologist and Play Therapist with 15+ years of professional experience working with children, adolescents, parents and families.

Her areas of professional work include child development, behavioural concerns, developmental and learning difficulties, psychological assessment, play therapy, early intervention, remedial training and parent and family counselling.

She founded AIDER Rehab Services in October 2021 with the vision of creating a professional yet warm environment where children can receive individualized support and parents can feel understood, guided and involved in the intervention process.
